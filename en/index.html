About Us

ABOUT US

CNC Machining · Professional Welding

Since 1983, Yijiahsin Co., Ltd. has specialized in the design, development, machining and manufacture of metal hardware, automotive parts and motorcycle components. Based in Yongkang District, Tainan, Taiwan, we combine in-house welding, CNC machining and inspection capabilities to support manufacturing projects across a range of industries.

Our factory is equipped with CNC milling machines, CNC lathes, welding machines and related production equipment. Dimensional inspection is carried out using coordinate measuring equipment. In addition to automotive and motorcycle applications, we also serve the medical equipment and sporting goods sectors.
